Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026. At TheSumitSharma.in, the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us.

Client-Side Processing (Zero Uploads)

The majority of our native web tools operate entirely within your browser's local memory. File processing occurs client-side, meaning your personal documents, texts, and files are never uploaded to our servers. We do not have access to the data you process using these native tools.

Log Files & Analytics

Like many other standard websites, this platform utilizes standard hosting log files. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This information is used to analyze trends, administer the site, and track user movement around the site. IP addresses and other such information are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

If you wish to disable cookies, you may do so through your individual browser options. More detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites.
