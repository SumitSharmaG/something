Information handled by this static site

This directory is designed as static pages and does not add an account system, database or contact form. Your hosting provider may process technical request data such as IP address, browser details and access logs. Confirm the provider's current practices and retention terms.

External destinations

Links to external services take you to sites operated by other parties. Those services may collect information under their own privacy notices and terms. Review the destination's policies before using it; this directory does not control embedded or linked services.

Cookies and advertising

No live advertising script or ad tag is included in this starter. If advertising such as Google AdSense is added later, third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalise ads, subject to the configured product and applicable settings. Verify the current vendor documentation, consent obligations and regional requirements before enabling it.
