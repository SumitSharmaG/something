About

A workbench for useful ideas.

Sumit Sharma Tools is a personal home for browser utilities, study resources and small engineering experiments.

What lives here

The collection spans full-stack applications, distributed real-time utilities, frontend experimentation and engineering guides. Some entries point to independently hosted projects; native workspaces in this starter are explicitly unfinished concepts.

How to use the directory

Search or filter the collection, open a guide to understand its scope and limitations, then follow a launch link where one is available. External projects operate under their own availability and policies.

Built by Sumit Sharma

This is an independent maker project. Follow ongoing work through GitHub. For university-related services, confirm official details with the institution.
